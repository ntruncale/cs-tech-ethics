CS Tech Ethics

A self-paced computer science unit for freshmen on data, privacy and fairness: 6 modules, then a film study of Fantastic Four: First Steps. Open the module your teacher assigned in mySPS, work through every step, then complete the mySPS check.

How it works

  • Every module has the same 7 steps: Context, Learn, Practice, Build, Reflect, Check, Go Further.
  • Activities check themselves. If you're stuck, tap Show a hint.
  • Some modules have a private self-check. Only you see your answers.
  • Watch for the 🦸 film notes. Each module ends with a question to keep in mind when you watch the movie.
  • Finish every activity to get the completion code for the mySPS check.

🔒 Unlock a module

Modules open when your teacher gives you their unlock code. Enter a code to open its module. You only need to enter each code once on this iPad.

Something online worrying you? If someone is pressuring or threatening you, or sharing private images of you, stop responding, save the evidence and tell a trusted adult: a parent, teacher or the school counselor. It's not your fault. Take It Down (takeitdown.ncmec.org) can help remove images of minors. If you or a friend are in crisis, call or text 988. In an emergency, call 911.
