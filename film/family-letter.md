# Family Letter Template: Film Viewing

*Edit the bracketed parts, then send it home or post it in mySPS about a week before the viewing. Check first whether your school requires a signed permission form for PG-13 films.*

Dear Families,

In our 9th-grade computer science class, students are finishing a unit called **CS Tech Ethics**. Over six modules they have learned about their digital footprint, how companies collect and use data, how to protect their accounts, how biased data can make unfair algorithms, the laws that protect privacy, and why privacy is a human right.

To bring these ideas together, the class will watch ***The Fantastic Four: First Steps*** (Marvel Studios, 2025) on **[dates]** during class. The film is about a world-famous family whose private life, including the birth of their son, becomes the whole world's business. Students will analyze it through the privacy ideas they've studied, then complete a written reflection and a short quiz.

**About the film:** It is rated **PG-13**. It includes superhero action and peril, some language, and themes of pregnancy, childbirth and a threat to a baby.

If you would prefer that your child not watch the film, please let me know by **[date]**. They will complete an alternative assignment covering the same ideas, with no effect on their grade.

Thank you for your support. Please reach out with any questions.

Sincerely,

[Teacher name]
